This work is something I'm quite excited about - I do a lot of work exploring the operation of the brain and more recently I have being doing more with radar so when my colleague Anum asked me to co-author a paper on neuromorphic radar it seemed like the perfect work.

Anum has done a great job producing a simulator to accompany the paper and I thoroughly recommend checking it out ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.24120190](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24120190){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}).
