I'm happy to announce that I have been elected as the Vice-Chair of the IEEE UK and Ireland Computer Society Chapter.

The Computer Society is one of the largest chapters in the UK and as such we have a wide range of members with varied interests and I am looking forward to working with the rest of the committee make sure we are representing those interests and continue to develop the chapter.
